# Apps docs

**Get started.** [Install](get-started/install.md) the apps beside the
libraries they use, then [render a MIDI file](get-started/first-program.md)
through the sound module.

**Guides.** [Install an app on a board](guides/install-an-app.md),
[play the sound module from a DAW](guides/play-the-sound-module.md),
[run Ostinato](guides/run-ostinato.md),
[put Vamp on a board](guides/put-vamp-on-a-board.md), and
[put a clock and the weather on your desk](guides/put-a-clock-on-your-desk.md).

**Reference.** [The apps, and what each does](reference/apps.md). [What an appkit app must provide](reference/app-requirements.md). [The icons an app can use](reference/icons.md). [The sound module's MIDI](reference/sound-module-midi.md): banks,
controllers, System Exclusive, and the `soundmodule.run` options. Vamp's
options are listed at the top of
[`programs/vamp/main.py`](../programs/vamp/main.py).

**How it works.** [How the apps are built](how-it-works/apps.md), [what is in the apps](how-it-works/overview.md) and
[how it is checked](how-it-works/how-its-checked.md).

**Changelog.** [CHANGELOG.md](../CHANGELOG.md). What is planned is in
